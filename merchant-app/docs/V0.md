# Ek Photo Dukaan — product V0

**Unofficial hackathon prototype.** Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or shipped by Paytm. No real Paytm APIs, production credentials, or live money movement.

Hackathon: Paytm AI Hackathon · Hyderabad · Theme 2 (AI for Small Businesses).  
Concept source: repo-root `Paytm_Ek_Photo_Dukaan_Brief.md`.  

---

## Problem

Every inventory app starts by asking the owner to type items, prices, and stock. A person with a queue at the counter will not do that twice. Data entry is a second unpaid job.

Without a catalogue, Paytm amounts the shop already collects cannot be interpreted as “what sold.” Without payments, a photo of the shelf is OCR with nowhere to act.

**Product line for judges:** every inventory app asks you to enter stock; nobody does; we never ask.

---

## User (kirana / counter)

**Meena Reddy** — Meena Kirana & General Store, Ameerpet, Hyderabad. One pair of hands, a printed rate card or shelf the customer can see, and Paytm collections she already takes.

She does not want a dashboard. She wants a price list that exists after one photo, a link or QR she can show a customer, and a restock hint before the best-seller is gone.

The longer-form brief uses a tea-stall (Ramesh) for identifiability of a 10–15 item menu. **V0 ships the kirana shell we already seeded** (Meena) with a small, editable list. Do not promise a 2,000-SKU kirana.

## One-photo flow (V0)

V0 is **one photograph**, not the full two-photo loop.

```text
Empty dukaan (no typing forms)
        │
        ▼
One photo: shelf / printed rate card / counter list
        │
        ▼
VisionService → VisionResult (items, prices, stock *ranges*, confidence, disclosure)
        │
        ▼
Merchant reviews + edits (authoritative)
        │
        ▼
Publish catalog → customer price list + QR / copy / WhatsApp draft
        │
        ▼
Restock hint from catalog flags + seeded Paytm ticket amounts
```

**Photo two (supplier invoice) and Bayesian stock-from-amounts** are the product north star in the brief. They were **out of the original V0 slice**. The judged build now also includes a **sample supplier bill**, merchant-confirmed baskets, rule-based days-of-cover, and a simulated reorder / stock-in — still labelled DEMO, still no bank API.

## In scope (must show)

- Paytm-for-Business-style **mobile-width** shell (existing app).
- Camera or upload of **one printed** shelf / rate-card image.
- Demo vision that returns 6–10 (up to ~15) SKUs with **price + stock range**, never a fake exact count.
- Honest UI: upload → reading → catalog; **demo model / prototype** labels.
- Merchant can edit name, price, availability; add/remove lines.
- Customer-facing price list (`/#/dukaan/:slug`), **QR**, copy link, WhatsApp-ready draft (opens a message; we do not send via Cloud API in V0).
- Restock **hint** on Smart insights / dukaan (visual low/out flags + payment amount matches).
- Demo reset returns to **empty catalog**; payments seed stays.
- Offline-capable demo: OCR assets (worker, WASM, language model) are served from our own origin, plus explicitly tapped sample shops. There is **no filename heuristic** — a sample is only ever loaded by tapping it.

## Out of scope (say this out loud)

- Production OCR / live VLM as the judged path (adapter exists for later).
- Handwritten rate cards.
- 2,000-SKU kirana; overlapping-price amount decomposition as a research demo.
- Handwritten invoices. The stage path still uses the matching sample bill so the reorder total is repeatable; a real photo is available on `/#/dukaan/invoice`.
- Exact inventory integers; “the model learned today” curves.
- Chatbot / “ask your inventory.”
- Real Paytm charge, payout, or webhook.
- Native WhatsApp Payments (Paytm is not in that India PG list).
- Autonomous money movement (merchant always approves).
- GST, loans, ads, consumer BBPS.

Cash sales are a known blind spot: **Paytm amounts are observed; cash is a prior.**

## 3-minute script for judges

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Home, empty dukaan | “Meena guesses what’s on the shelf. Saturday, Thums Up is gone.” |
| 0:20–0:40 | Product name | “Every app says enter your stock. We ask for one photo.” |
| 0:40–1:20 | Photo → catalog | “Thirty seconds. That’s the price list. She typed nothing.” |
| 1:20–1:50 | Edit one line, publish | “She stays in control. The photo is a draft.” |
| 1:50–2:20 | Customer list + QR / share | “Customer sees prices. She already collects on Paytm.” |
| 2:20–2:45 | Restock insight | “Low stock from the photo, matched to what people already pay.” |
| 2:45–3:00 | Close | “One photo. After that the shop has a dukaan. Invoice + payments come next.” |

If the camera path is flaky: tap **Meena's kirana shelf** under *or start from a sample shop*, or one of the sample photos under *or read a sample photo for real* (see `DEMO.md`). Never stall on live handwriting.

## Success criteria

1. A photo (or seeded sample) produces a useful editable catalog in **under ~10 seconds** with **zero typing** on the happy path.
2. Merchant edits are **API-authoritative**; refresh still shows the catalog; Profile → **Advanced** → **Reset to sample data** clears the catalog consistently.
3. Customer view, QR, and share text reflect the same catalog.
4. Unknown / unreadable images are **refused with a reason**, never turned into invented rows. There is no silent fallback catalog: if the read finds nothing, the screen says so and offers a sample shop or a fresh photo.
5. Restock copy is a **hint**, not an exact count.
6. Prototype / unofficial Paytm disclaimer is visible in the UI header or dukaan screens.
7. `cd merchant-app && npm run dev` is enough for the demo; `npm run build` still typechecks the frontend.
