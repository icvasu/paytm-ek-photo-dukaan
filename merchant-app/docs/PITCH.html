The reason every inventory app fails
400

items on Meena's
shelves.

Every app starts by asking her to type all of them. At twenty seconds each that is two hours she does not have, and prices change, so it is never done once.

She is not lazy. Data entry is a second job that pays nothing. So she guesses, and on Saturday evening the Thums Up is gone before she notices.

Setup

One photo. No typing.

Input

A photo of the shelf

Or the rate card already taped to the wall. Taken on her own phone, read on her own phone.

→
On device

Text becomes a catalog

OCR reads the lines, then fuzzy matching turns them into named items with prices. Nothing leaves the phone.

→
Output

A live dukaan

  • Tata Salt 1 kg 28
  • Parle-G 250 g 25
  • Thums Up 750 ml 45
  • Fortune Oil 1 L 142

Plus a public price list and a scannable UPI QR, so the same photo that built her catalog also gets her paid.

The part nobody else can copy

Payments keep
the register.

01

A customer pays ₹45

A number, not a scan. No item was rung up.

02

We solve for the basket

Search every combination of her prices that sums to exactly ₹45. Fourteen searched, one fits.

03

She confirms in one tap

The model proposes. The shopkeeper decides. Stock never moves on a guess.

04

The shelf estimate drops

Thums Up goes from two to three left, down to one to two, with two days of cover.

Amount alone can never prove a basket, so confidence is capped at 92 percent and every suggestion shows its runners up. That honesty is what makes the number usable.

Why this has to be Paytm

Swap out Paytm and
the product disappears.

Sensor

Paytm QR and soundbox

The amount stream. This is the only place stock going out is observed without asking her to type.

Cold start

Paytm for Business camera

Shelf and supplier bill read on device, inside an app she already opens every morning.

Inference

Basket solver

Turns amounts into units sold, bounded by what the supplier bill says actually came in.

Action

Paytm vendor payout

Reorder is a real payment to a real supplier, drafted on WhatsApp where the order already happens.

A standalone catalog app has no sensor and no way to act. It would ask her to type her sales, which is the product we are refusing to build.

What we will not pretend

Built today, and
mocked today.

Real and running

  • OCR on device, no server, no model key
  • Bounded subset sum basket solver with audited search counts
  • UPI intent QR to the NPCI upi://pay spec. Scan it on your own phone
  • API backed catalog, stock and supplier orders that survive a refresh
  • WhatsApp draft that opens with the order written

Simulated, and labelled

  • Paytm order and settlement webhooks
  • WhatsApp Cloud API delivery
  • Production grade vision model
  • Calibrated forecasting. Today it is a weighted rate, capped at 85 percent
  • Supplier payout. Nothing touches a bank
  • Payment references. Ours, and the receipt says App payment reference, not UPI transaction ID

Also honest: cash sales are a blind spot, and the demo shop is a representative kirana, not production data. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Paytm, stated in the app under Profile then About. Every simulated screen says so on the screen itself, not in a footnote.
