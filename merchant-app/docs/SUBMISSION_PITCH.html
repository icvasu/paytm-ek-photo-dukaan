05 / 09
AI innovation · four explainable components

Intelligence that refuses instead of guessing.

01 · Perception

On-device OCR

Tesseract.js reads printed text in the browser. No upload, API key, or per-call fee.

02 · Resolution

Fuzzy name match

Repairs likely spelling errors against a product lexicon without adding unseen products.

03 · Inference

Bounded subset-sum

Searches exact-price baskets within strict item, quantity, and compute limits.

04 · Prediction

Weighted demand

Confirmed baskets become velocity, days of cover, and reorder suggestions.

No text?

No catalog.

No exact sum?

Ask the merchant.

Several sums?

Show choices; never decrement silently.

Rule-based, bounded, and auditable. Every displayed confidence has a stated ceiling.
